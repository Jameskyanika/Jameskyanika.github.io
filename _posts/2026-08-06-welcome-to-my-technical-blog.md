Welcome to my technical blog.

This blog will share practical knowledge and real-world lessons from my experience in networking, systems administration, cybersecurity, virtualization and IT support.

## Topics I Will Cover

The articles will include practical guidance on:

- Enterprise networking
- Windows desktop and server support
- FortiGate firewalls
- Proxmox VE and VMware
- CCTV systems
- Printers and end-user support
- ISP connectivity and troubleshooting
- Cybersecurity best practices

## Why I Created This Blog

My goal is to document useful technical solutions, share troubleshooting steps and provide resources that can help IT professionals, students, businesses and technical support teams.

New articles will be published regularly.
